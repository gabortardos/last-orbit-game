## PT-001 — Prepared open-hand tutorial walkthrough

- Status: ready to run
- Rules: `LO-RULES-0.1`
- Players: 3
- Information: hands remain face up for teaching and diagnosis
- Deck recipe: exact three-player recipe from `deck-recipes.v0.1.json`
- Factions: cosmetic placeholders only; no powers

### Hypotheses

1. Players can understand the three-stage turn after one guided example.
2. Two Commands allow a plan without producing excessive turn length.
3. Deep Scan meaningfully combines with Course Correction or Vector Flip.
4. Forced Detour creates aggression without giving the commander a free escape.
5. Signal Jam and Decoy Beacon resolve without timing disputes.
6. Emergency Burn provides limited relief from Pressure.
7. Crisis and Emergency Reroute resolution is memorable and unambiguous.

### Required staged situations

- Deep Scan followed by Course Correction.
- Deep Scan followed by Vector Flip.
- Forced Detour resolved normally.
- Forced Detour cancelled by Signal Jam.
- Targeted Command redirected by Decoy Beacon, followed by the new target's choice.
- Multi-Pressure Flight Phase using Emergency Burn.
- Crisis draw with Emergency Reroute.
- Crisis draw without Reroute and elimination.
- Final-two-player victory check.

### Pass criteria

- No invented ruling is required.
- Turn stages can be repeated correctly by all participants.
- Reaction ownership is always clear.
- Players identify at least one meaningful pre-draw decision.
- No ordinary resolution exceeds one minute after teaching.

### Failure triggers

- Players repeatedly forget current or Queued Pressure.
- Two Commands feel arbitrary or restrictive without adding strategy.
- Emergency Reroute clearing remaining Pressure removes too much tension.
- Decoy Beacon produces unclear or unsatisfying outcomes.
- Open information still cannot make a rule understandable.
