## Required record for every test

- Test ID and rules version
- Date and facilitator
- Player count and participant experience
- Hypothesis
- Included and excluded modules
- Exact deck recipe
- Match duration and turn count
- First-elimination time
- Longest eliminated-player wait
- Card usage and unused cards
- Confusing or disputed moments
- Strongest and weakest decisions
- Fun, tension, clarity, agency, humour, and replay-interest ratings
- Observations
- Decision: pass, revise, rebuild, or stop
