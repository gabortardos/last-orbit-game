# Visual Direction

Status: principles only; no final art direction selected.

## Current tonal target

- Approximately 60% impressive and cinematic
- Approximately 30% character comedy
- Approximately 10% outright absurdity

Comedy should often emerge after a threat initially appears spectacular and credible.

## Requirements

- Strong faction silhouettes
- Cool planetary materials and environments
- Readable cards and targets on mobile
- Animation that explains outcomes
- Skippable recurring sequences
- Reduced-motion support
- Colour reinforced by icons, shapes, and labels
- No generic neon science-fiction dashboard
- No imitation of the reference game's visual language

## Directions to compare later

1. Selective Solar System Explorer rendering reuse.
2. New stylized 3D planetary world.
3. Hybrid 2D cards and characters with a 3D event stage.
4. Cinematic 2.5D presentation.

Do not commission finished assets before the rules show sufficient promise.
