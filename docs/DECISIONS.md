# Decision Log

## DEC-001 — Independent project

- Date: 25 October 2026
- Status: accepted
- Decision: Last Orbit is a separate project, repository, documentation set, and eventual deployment from Solar System Explorer.
- Reason: protect the Explorer and allow the game to develop according to its own requirements.

## DEC-002 — Browser multiplayer is the intended product

- Date: 25 October 2026
- Status: accepted
- Decision: the intended product is a browser-accessible online multiplayer game; text/card prototypes are validation tools.
- Consequence: future architecture must support authoritative multiplayer and responsive desktop/mobile interfaces.

## DEC-003 — Creative mandate

- Date: 25 October 2026
- Status: accepted
- Decision: earlier prompts and proposals are rough guidance. Better ideas may replace them.
- Constraint: confirmed owner requirements and test evidence must be preserved or explicitly superseded.

## DEC-004 — Presentation ambition

- Date: 25 October 2026
- Status: accepted
- Decision: cool graphics, characters, spaceships, cinematic events, and animation are eventual product requirements.
- Consequence: presentation is planned early but produced after gameplay validation.

## DEC-005 — Explorer reuse remains undecided

- Date: 25 October 2026
- Status: accepted
- Decision: compare selective Explorer reuse with a new visual world after the digital interaction requirements are known.

## DEC-006 — Multiple Crisis identities

- Date: 25 October 2026
- Status: experimental
- Decision: v0.1 uses multiple narratively distinct Crisis cards with the same mechanical resolution.
- Reason: creative variety without premature balance complexity.

## DEC-007 — First rules-test constraints

- Date: 25 October 2026
- Status: experimental
- Decision: test five support cards plus one Emergency Reroute, two Commands per normal turn, and a maximum Flight Pressure of four.
- Evidence required: scripted validation and human playtests.

## DEC-008 — Continuity through repository state

- Date: 25 October 2026
- Status: accepted
- Decision: future models must be able to continue from canonical repository documents without conversation history.
