# Civilization Bible

Status: early concept directions only. No faction is final.

| Origin | Working civilization | Creative direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mercury | Flareskips | Heat-sail speedsters burdened by cooling failures |
| Venus | Cloud Courtiers | Acid-cloud social strategists in ornate protective forms |
| Earth | Improvisers | Humans using incompatible technology, adapters, and optimism |
| Mars | Rustborn | Competitive mechanical engineers and drill enthusiasts |
| Jupiter | Stormkin | Large atmospheric beings in containment systems |
| Saturn | Ring Corsairs | Crystalline collectors with personal orbiting structures |
| Uranus | Sideways Sages | Ice beings with a radically different idea of orientation |
| Neptune | Deepwave Oracles | Bioluminescent prophets whose warnings are technically correct |

## Requirements for every final civilization

- Distinct biology or constructed form
- Memorable captain and silhouette
- Flagship identity
- Homeworld-derived concept
- Strength, weakness, and strategic identity
- Comic contradiction deeper than one joke
- Colour, material, animation, and sound language
- Avoidance of generic grey/green alien design

Faction powers are excluded from the first core prototype.
