# Rules

Status: v0.1 not yet drafted.

## Locked prototype assumptions

- 2–5 players.
- Last operational flagship wins.
- A normal turn will allow up to two Commands, followed by one Flight Deck draw unless an effect ends the current obligation.
- Each player starts with one Emergency Reroute.
- The Flight Deck contains one Crisis fewer than the number of players.
- Crises share one resolution in the first test.
- Emergency Reroute survives a Crisis and secretly returns it anywhere in the Flight Deck.
- No faction powers or Reactor Charge in the first test.

## Required before v0.1 can be marked complete

- Setup for every player count.
- Complete card taxonomy and exact counts.
- Turn, action, target, reaction, resolution, draw, and cleanup timing.
- Flight Pressure rules.
- Crisis and Emergency Reroute resolution.
- Theft, transfer, scan, shuffle, and cancellation rules.
- Elimination and endgame.
- Public and private information.
- Empty-hand, empty-deck, simultaneous-reaction, and invalid-action handling.
- Worked examples.
